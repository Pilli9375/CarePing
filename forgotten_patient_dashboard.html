🔬 Unified Processing Pipeline

Laboratory (D07) and Radiology (D06) do not have separated STAT queues. Urgent blood and CT tests enter the exact same FIFO line as routine outpatient tests.

📉 Severe Daily Capacity Crunch

⏱️ Absence of 30-Minute SLA Triggers

Forensic Synthetic Data Quality & Schema Integrity Audit

Addressing the Datathon README caveat: "teams should check data quality and avoid treating synthetic associations as clinical truth."

6 Anomalies Verified
Desynchronized Timestamps 4,998 Records (99.96%)

Only 2 of 5,000 diagnostic tests occurred inside the patient's visit window. 2,481 tests occurred before arrival and 2,517 after discharge.

• Proves diagnostic test timestamps were generated independently of patient visit dates.

"None" CSV String Trap 747 Patients (37.35%)

Standard pandas parser misinterprets literal string "None" as NaN null values. Setting keep_default_na=False confirms 0.0% true null values.

• Avoided incorrect data imputation trap.

Ghost Service on LWBS 242 Encounters (100%)

All 242 walkout encounters still log full service_start_time, service_end_time, and service_minutes (mean 47.4m), plus 148 lab tests.

• Hospital EHR needs schema constraints blocking service timestamps on walkouts.

Pediatric Clinical Routing 533 Adults (81.13%)

In Pediatrics (D03), patient ages range up to 95 years old (mean age 47.36). 81.1% of patients in Pediatrics are adults aged >18.

• Synthetic assignment lacked clinical age range constraint.

Unseen Registered Cohort 45 Patients (2.25%)

45 registered patients have zero encounters in 2025. 26 of them (57.8%) have chronic conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, Cardiac).

• Registry leakage requires proactive community outreach.

Demographic Non-Confounding Eta² < 0.30% (p > 0.30)

ANOVA confirms patient age, sex, insurance type, and chronic condition do not statistically influence wait times or walkouts.

• Queue mechanics dominate; demographics are synthetic noise.
